Gemstone Cam

Only the stone inside the dashed box is measured. Keep the lighting and distance the same every time.

Identify

—

Teach some stones first, then press Identify (or the BOOT button on the board).

Teach

Teach each stone 5–10 times, turning it a little between presses. Also teach "empty" with nothing in the box.

Camera settings

Set these up before teaching. Turning off auto white balance and auto exposure keeps colours consistent between teaching and identifying. Settings are remembered after a reboot.
